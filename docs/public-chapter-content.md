# Public chapter stories

The public archive at `/activities` uses `config/chapter_stories.php`, with standalone pages at `/activities/{slug}`. It deliberately does not pull private projects, reports, membership data, or demo-seeded events into the public website.

Each entry has an activity date for ordering, a human-readable date label, a topic, an image (optional), a source URL, and a separate source publication label. Month-only and multi-month activities use a first-of-month sort key; the displayed label preserves the known precision. Existing public-page sources and local images were reused for the January–July 2026 stories. The PNVSCA July forum report was accessible and checked on 25 October 2026.

Facebook and Instagram did not expose the chapter timeline during this update. Do not describe this collection as a complete record through October. Source publication dates in August do not mean the activities took place in August.

Add a full article after obtaining its official description, date, source post, and appropriate media. No project outcome, participant count, or scheduled date should be inferred from the name.
